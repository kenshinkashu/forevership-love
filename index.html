Kuch lafz
sirf tumhare liye.

"Mohabbat sirf paane ka naam nahi,
kisi ki khushi mein apni khushi dhoondhne ka naam hai.
Tum paas ho ya door, farq bas itna sa hai,
mera dil aaj bhi tumhare naam se hi muskurata hai."

Zindagi mein bahut se log milte hain, lekin kuch log sirf milte nahi, hamari duniya ko khoobsurat bana dete hain. Tum mere liye unhi mein se ek ho. Tumhari ek chhoti si smile mere bure din ko bhi achha bana sakti hai. Tum important ho, sirf isliye nahi ki tum meri life mein ho, balki isliye ki tumhare hone se meri life aur bhi special lagti hai.
